# 🧭 ULRS1182 – Appreciation of Ethics and Civilizations

## 📘 Course Brief
This course introduces the concept of ethics from the perspective of different civilizations. It explores social development, cultural values, and moral systems across societies while helping students understand how ethics influences social harmony, citizenship, and national unity. The course also discusses contemporary issues in economics, politics, society, culture, and the environment from an ethical viewpoint.
