# 📊 SECP3213 Business Intelligence

## 📘 Course Brief
This course introduces the concepts, methodologies, and tools used to transform raw data into meaningful business insights. It covers business intelligence fundamentals, data warehousing, ETL processes, dashboard development, data visualization, and predictive analytics. Through hands-on projects, students learn how to integrate data from multiple sources and design interactive dashboards to support data-driven decision making.

### Data & AI Governance Industry Talk
Attended an industry sharing session titled **"Bridging Data & Decisions: The Role of Data Governance and Data Management in Modern Business Intelligence"** by **Faiz Fablillah**, Senior Principal of Enterprise Data & AI Governance at PIDM.

- 📄 [Data Governance Talk Reflection](./Industry%20Talk.pdf)

#### Key Takeaways
- Data governance is essential to ensure data accuracy, security, and trustworthiness.
- Business dashboards are only valuable when built upon reliable and well-managed data.
- Governance establishes accountability, ownership, compliance, and data quality standards.
- Technologies such as Master Data Management (MDM), data lineage tracking, metadata management, and data catalogs improve data reliability.
- AI and analytics initiatives require strong governance foundations to deliver trustworthy outcomes.

#### Reflection
This session broadened my perspective on the importance of data governance within modern analytics ecosystems. Previously, I viewed governance primarily as a security and privacy mechanism. However, the talk highlighted that governance is equally important in ensuring data quality and trustworthiness before data reaches dashboards or AI systems. I also learned that organizations must balance governance requirements with operational efficiency, especially in large-scale environments. Moving forward, I believe AI-assisted governance can help organizations maintain both data quality and performance.
