# 🦺 SAFETY AND HEALTH OFFICER (SHO) – Introductory Course

## 📘 Course Brief
This course introduces fundamental occupational safety and health (OSH) principles, focusing on workplace hazards, accident prevention, OSHA 1994 regulations, and safe working practices relevant to engineering and work environments.

---

# 📁 Assignments

- 📄 [Individual Task Report](./Individual%20Task%20(SAFETY%20AND%20HEALTH%20OFFICER%20INTRODUCTORY%20COURSE).pdf) – Completed tasks covering OSH concepts including accident cases, OSHA 1994 employer and employee duties, safety regulations, accident theories, unsafe acts vs conditions, and workplace safety guidelines.
