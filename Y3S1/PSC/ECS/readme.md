# 🇬🇧 English Communication Skills (ECS)

## 📘 Course Brief
This course focuses on assessing and improving English communication skills, including writing, speaking, discussion, and critical thinking. Students are evaluated on their ability to communicate effectively in both written and spoken English.

---

# 📁 Assessment

- 📄 [ECS Writing Test](./ECS%20Wrting%20Test%20(Goe%20Jie%20Ying%20A2CS0224).docx) – English proficiency assessment consisting of a writing test and a group speaking discussion where students communicated and exchanged ideas on a given topic.

# 💭 Reflection
- **What I learned:** I improved my confidence in expressing ideas through both written and spoken English.
- **Challenges:** Communicating fluently during group discussions and organizing ideas clearly within the writing task.
- **Takeaway:** Effective communication requires both language proficiency and the ability to present ideas clearly and confidently.
