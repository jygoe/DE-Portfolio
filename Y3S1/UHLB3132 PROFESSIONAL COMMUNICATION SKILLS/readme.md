# 🗣️ UHLB3132 – Professional Communication Skills 2

## 📘 Course Brief
This course focuses on advanced professional communication skills, including pitching, negotiation, persuasive writing, and business communication. Students learn how to present proposals, defend ideas, and communicate effectively in formal and professional business contexts.
