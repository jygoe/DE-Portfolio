# 📡 SKEF2123 – Instrumentation & Measurement

## 📘 Course Brief
This course introduces the fundamentals of instrumentation and measurement systems. It covers basic measurement principles, electrical sensing, calibration techniques, and signal interpretation. Students gain hands-on experience in analysing electrical circuits and understanding measurement accuracy using practical lab experiments.
