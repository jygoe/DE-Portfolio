# 📘 ULRS1032 - Integrity and Anti - Corruption Course

## 🧾 Course Brief  
This course introduces UTM’s core values of Integrity, Synergy, Excellence, and Sustainability (ISES) to develop creative and innovative students for national development. It emphasizes integrity, ethics, anti-corruption awareness, and strong personal values in both daily life and organizations. The course also highlights diversity, ethical decision-making, and holistic problem-solving of current issues, while aligning with Malaysia’s Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) and national development vision.

---

## 📝 Tugasan
- [Tugasan 1](./Tugasan%201%20(couple).pdf/) – A paired activity focusing on basic understanding and application of integrity-related concepts through discussion and problem solving.
- [Tugasan 2a](./Tugasan%202a%20(Kes%20Kajian%20Rasuah).pdf/) – A case study analyzing real-life corruption issues and identifying ethical problems and solutions.
- [Tugasan 2b](./Tugasan%202b%20(Poster%20Minggu%20Integriti).pdf/) – A poster design task to promote awareness about Integrity Week and highlight key moral values.
- [Tugasan 2c](./Tugasan%202c%20(Video%20PSA).md/) – A video PSA project to communicate messages on integrity and anti-corruption in a creative way.

## 💭 Reflection
- What I learned: I learned about the importance of integrity, ethics, and core values such as ISES in both personal life and professional environments. The course also introduced awareness of corruption prevention and the importance of responsible behavior in society.
- Challenges: Some concepts were more theoretical and required deeper understanding of real-life applications, especially in ethics and societal issues.
- Takeaway: This course helped me develop stronger moral values, awareness of social responsibility, and the importance of acting with integrity in everyday life and future career.
