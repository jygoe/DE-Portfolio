# 👩‍💻 SECP1513 – Technology and Information Systems

## 📘 Course Brief
This course introduces students to Information Systems and Technology (IS/IT) and its applications in daily life and workplace environments. It covers fundamental components such as hardware, software, networking, and communications. Students gain hands-on experience in PC installation, productivity tools, industry exposure through visits and talks, and basic career exploration in the IT field.
