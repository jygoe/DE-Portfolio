# 💡 SECD3761 – Technopreneurship Seminar

## 📘 Course Brief
This course introduces the fundamentals of technopreneurship through the planning and organization of events such as seminars, showcases, and marketing activities. Students develop leadership, communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills while gaining practical experience in event management, entrepreneurship, and industry engagement.
